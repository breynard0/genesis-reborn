welcome to genesis!

build an operating system from scratch, get the compute to run it on!

what's genesis?

genesis is a Hack Club YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) where teenagers can get free computers for building operating systems from scratch. It's also accompanied by a weekly LIVE LECTURE SERIES given by yours truly (guac)!!

are you for real??

yep! hack club is a real 501(c)(3) non-profit headquartered in California, and we've been running programs like this for quite a while now. We've run hackathons on private islands, at GitHub HQ, and took 30 teens to see Strandbeest in the Netherlands, all FREE for technical teenagers like you!

how do i get started?

get set up on Hackatime, our time-tracking platform, and get started on an operating system! it can be any language you want, but keep an eye out for my lecture series so we can write it together in C - the lectures will be streamed in Hack Club's Slack, and then they'll be uploaded to our YouTube channel and put here :)

who are you?

i'm guac! i'm a hack club gap year and an evil technomancer, and i love you. i made this website by hand for you! genesis is brought to you by myself and Brendan, one of Hack Club's incredible low-level sorcerers and the technical knowhow behind this whole program.
